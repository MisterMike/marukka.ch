# Marukka

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web, powered by Craft CMS 5, Vite, Tailwind CSS 4 and Alpine.js.

## Users

Developers looking for a small working example, a practical fix, or a technical note to return to later.

## Product Purpose

A personal collection of manually authored code snippets, short tutorials and development notes. The primary tasks are browsing topics, finding a snippet, reading its explanation, and copying code.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Homepage, topic archive, search, sorting, pagination and Matrix-based article detail. Teasers are authored independently of Matrix content and may be plain text or code. Content is entered manually; no legacy migration is in scope. Do not invent articles, reading times, subscription handling or contact/legal details.

## Brand Commitments

The supplied homepage and category-grid mockups in `src/mockups` are the visual authority. Use the provided hero and category artwork in `web/assets/template`. Preserve the dark navy, magenta/violet, thin borders and square geometry. Native CSS nesting and custom properties express the identity; Tailwind utilities handle basic layout only.

## Open Decisions

Confirm the site's publication language (the blueprint currently uses `de`, while the supplied mockups and implemented interface use English), legal pages, and final editorial copy before launch.
